A Slack-native AI assistant that puts the full sales and product knowledge base at a rep's fingertips — with confidence scoring on every answer.

Problem

Sales reps needed quick access to product and process answers inside Slack. Search was slow and knowledge scattered across docs.

What I built

An AI assistant that answers Slack questions using a RAG approach against product docs and playbooks. Confidence scores help reps know when to escalate to humans.

Impact

Reduced time to answer by ~40% and lowered support ticket volume for common questions.
