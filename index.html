Deal Risk Intelligence System

Deals were dying after reaching advanced stages despite strong early pipeline. Analysis of 30+ lost deals surfaced consistent patterns. Built an LLM-powered engine to surface risks before the weekly pipeline review.

Role: Lead Revenue Operations Industry: B2B SaaS Q4 2025 · 4 weeks
hero — dashboard before / after
Client
B2B SaaS · Series A · AR Automation
Scope
Deal risk scoring & pipeline intelligence
Stack
Claude API · HubSpot API · JavaScript
Timeline
Q4 2025 · 4 weeks
Headline outcome
65%
forecast accuracy — up from 30% at baseline

Context

A Series A B2B SaaS team with $6M quarterly pipeline. Deals were consistently dying at advanced stages — technical evaluation, pricing, contracts — despite strong early momentum. An analysis of 30+ lost deals (~$1M in pipeline) surfaced three consistent patterns: absent economic buyers, weak compelling events, and inactive internal champions.

The problem

The sales head needed a way to surface these risks early enough to act — not after the deal was already lost. Gut-feel pipeline reviews were the only tool available, and they weren’t working.

“The hardest part wasn’t the build. It was getting the sales head to trust the model’s read on a deal over his own gut feel.”

What I built

An LLM-powered deal risk engine using Claude and the HubSpot API. The system reads across call transcripts, AE notes, prospect emails, and conversation history — combined with structured signals like stage duration and contract value — and scores each deal against key qualification dimensions.

For every deal, it outputs three things: identified risks, pressure-test questions for the next pipeline review, and historical similarity matches to past lost deals. Sales leadership used this directly in weekly pipeline calls, replacing gut-feel with an evidence-based conversation.

data model — dbt lineage
looker exec dashboard

Impact

One quarter after launch, the forecast was within 6% of actuals — and Monday mornings got their half-day back.

Reflection

Call transcripts were the highest-signal input — richer than any structured CRM field. The lasting shift wasn’t the tool itself — it was moving weekly pipeline reviews from AE self-reporting to evidence-based conversations.

Finn AI

A Slack-native AI assistant that puts Growfin's entire sales and product knowledge base at a rep's fingertips — with a confidence tag on every answer so they know how much to trust it before using it with a customer.

PythonSlack APILLM / RAGSalesforce

My role
Design + build, solo
Stack
Claude API · Slack · RAG pipeline · Growfin docs library
Status
Live, in daily use

The problem

No scalable way for reps to self-serve knowledge in the flow of work. Product Marketing was a bottleneck, and the internal knowledge base lived in scattered Notion pages no one opened mid-call. AE, BDR, and CS all had the same problem: wrong answer at the wrong moment, or no answer at all.

What it does

  • Answers product and competitive questions in-channel.
    @finn what does Growfin do better than HighRadius?
  • Returns answers with a confidence tag — High / Medium / Low — so reps know how much to trust it before using it with a prospect.
  • Eliminates the Product Marketing bottleneck for in-call enablement queries — across both sales and post-sales.

How it works

Reps post a natural language question in the #finn-ai Slack channel. The system retrieves relevant chunks from Growfin's internal knowledge base via a RAG pipeline, generates a response using Claude, and returns it with a confidence tag — so reps know how much to trust the answer.
